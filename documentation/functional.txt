**7.2 Data Ingestion:**
Step 1: Ingest ECC sales order delta data into a bronze table.

**7.3 Data Transformation:**

1. Create a lookup table based on the join condition of ship-to country and customer classification values.
2. Derive sales office value using the lookup table.
3. Segregate sales orders into Revenue, Non-Rev, or Customer Consignment based on Sales Doc Type.
4. Update organizational elements, master data attributes, transactional data quantities, units of measure, and quantity conversions to the EA (Each) unit of measure.
