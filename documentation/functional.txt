Here is the detailed Functional Requirement Document (FRD) based on the provided Business Requirement Document (BRD):

**1.0 Purpose**

The purpose of this project is to support accurate global demand planning by ingesting ABC historical sales order data, enriching it with regional sales organization and plant mapping data, and generating extracts for consumption by downstream systems.

**2.0 Data Product Design**

**2.1 Data Sources**

The following input files are required for this project:

1. **JDA-ABC 5 years Order Demand History** ( flat file)
   |Column Name|Data Type|
   |DMDUNIT|CHAR(40)|
   |DMDGROUP|CHAR(10)|
   |LOC|CHAR(3)|
   |STARTDATE|DATE|
   |DUR|NUMERIC|
   |TYPE|CHAR(1)|
   |EVENT|CHAR(10)|
   |QTY|NUMERIC|
   |HISTSTREAM|CHAR(4)|

2. **JDA - ABC Regional, sales orgs & plant mapping data** (flat file)
   |Column Name|Data Type|
   |Product|CHAR(40)|
   |LOC|CHAR(3)|
   |REGION/Sales Org|CHAR(4)|
   |COUNTRY|CHAR(3)|
   |CHANNEL|CHAR(10)|
   |BD sales org|CHAR(4)|
   |BD Plant|CHAR(4)|

**2.2 Data Ingestion**

The following data ingestion is required:

|ABC_sales_order|abfss://user-managed@{storage_account}.dfs.core.windows.net/inbound/xyz/ABC_onetime_history/ABC_sales_order|b_um_xyz.ABC_onetime_history_sales_orders|
|ABC_sales_org_plant_xref|abfss://user-managed@{storage_account}.dfs.core.windows.net/inbound/xyz/ABC_onetime_history/ABC_sales_org_plant_xref|b_um_xyz.ABC_onetime_history_sales_org_plant_xref|

**2.3 Data Transformation**

The data transformation steps are as follows:

1. Ingest data from the source files into the bronze tables.
2. Create a silver table `s_xyz.sales_orders_demand_fcst_ABC_onetime_history` by joining the bronze tables on the product and LOC columns.
   * Enrich the dataset with regional sales organization and plant mapping data.
   * Apply business rules to populate key fields such as Sales Organization, Region, Sales Office, Plant, and Planning Partner.
3. Create a gold view `g_external.v_sales_orders_demand_fcst_ABC_onetime_history` from the silver table.
